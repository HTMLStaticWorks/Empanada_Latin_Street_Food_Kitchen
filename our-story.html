Where It Began

From Street Markets To Your Neighborhood Counter

In 2021, we set up our very first pop-up counter in Barrio Central equipped with just a wooden rolling pin, an old brass oven, and our grandmother’s secret sofrito recipe.

What started as a weekend pop-up serving 100 empanadas a day grew rapidly into a beloved street-food destination. Today, while our kitchens have expanded across multiple locations, our core promise remains unchanged: every single empanada is hand-folded every single morning.

Core Principles

Our Food Philosophy

Four sacred rules guide every batch that comes out of our stone ovens each morning.

1. The Sacred Repulgue

We reject automated dough presses. Every empanada receives our signature 13-fold repulgue braid by hand, creating airy pockets that seal in natural braising juices.

2. 3-Hour Fresh Sofrito

At 5:00 AM daily, we chop fresh sweet bell peppers, red onions, garlic, cumin, and Mexican oregano. Simmered slow in olive oil, it forms the soul of every meat filling.

3. Stone Deck Baking

Rather than soaking in heavy fryer fats, our empanadas bake at 425°F on stone hearth oven decks. The result is a flaky, blistered golden crust with genuine flavor clarity.

4. Sourced With Respect

We work directly with regional family ranches and dairy farms for pasture-raised beef, Oaxaca melting cheese, and organic herbs. Zero additives, zero artificial dyes.
