Quality First

What Makes Our Empanadas Famous

We don't do shortcuts. From daily hand-crimped dough to slow-simmered sofrito bases, flavor is baked into every layer.

Butter Flaky Dough

Kneaded fresh every morning using real farm butter and baked or fried to crispy, golden perfection.

12-Hour Sofrito Marinade

Garlic, sweet peppers, cilantro, oregano, and achiote simmered slowly to create rich, authentic Latin depth.

House-Made Chimichurri

Fresh parsley, garlic, red wine vinegar, and extra virgin olive oil mixed fresh every single afternoon.
