The Casa Advantage

Why Choose Our Catering

We take the stress out of event planning with hot, prompt delivery, handcrafted flavor, and seamless presentation.

Piping Hot Delivery

Delivered in heavy-duty commercial thermal cambros ensuring your empanadas remain flaky, crispy, and oven-hot upon arrival.

100% Hand-Crimped

Never frozen or factory made. Every pastry shell is braided by hand in our commercial scratch kitchen on the morning of your event.

Dietary Clarity

Every platter and box is clearly labeled for meat, vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-aware diets to make serving guests effortless.

Eco Serving Setup

Includes sustainable palm leaf plates, unbleached napkins, stainless serving tongs, and recyclable dip containers at zero extra charge.
